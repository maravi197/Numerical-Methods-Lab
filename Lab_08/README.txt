Lab 08
Numerical differentiation

Finite differences approximate derivatives from tabulated values. Use central differences where points are available on both sides; Richardson extrapolation combines two step sizes.

Each Q file is a separate program. Open Q1.cpp, Q2.cpp, or Q3.cpp and follow main() from top to bottom.
Compile one file at a time: g++ -std=c++17 Q1.cpp -o Q1
